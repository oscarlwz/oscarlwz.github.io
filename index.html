01 / Perspective

Black holes are small.
Their influence is not.

My work follows matter as it flows into—and is expelled from—accreting black holes. These winds connect scales from the central parsec to an entire galaxy.

Using integral-field spectroscopy from JWST, HST, MUSE, and ground-based observatories, I map multiphase gas and test how feedback regulates star formation and black-hole growth across cosmic time.
